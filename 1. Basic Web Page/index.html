Few facts about the world we live in!

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. It is covered by vast oceans, mountains, forests, deserts, and diverse ecosystems. Earth has a unique atmosphere containing oxygen and other gases that make life possible. Approximately 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water, while the remaining 29% consists mainly of land. Our planet takes about 365 days to complete one revolution around the Sun and about 24 hours to rotate once on its axis.

About Earth:

  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun and is about 4.5 billion years old.
  • About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by oceans, making it the only known world with large amounts of liquid surface water.
  • Earth's atmosphere and magnetic field help protect life from harmful radiation and extreme conditions in space.
  • For more info: NASA

Top 3 Facts about Earth:

  1. Earth is the only planet currently known to support life.
  2. Earth's surface is covered by about 71% water.
  3. Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon.
